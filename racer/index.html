Privacy Policy

Application: Endless Arcade • Package ID: com.aashiqhasnat.endlessarcade • Effective Date & Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Endless Arcade is an arcade racing and vehicular combat game developed by Ashik Hasnat ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Endless Arcade on Android or Web.

1. Local-Only Game Storage (No Developer Servers)

We do not operate proprietary user-tracking servers and do not directly collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or personal files.

All gameplay progress—including high scores, unlocked vehicles, upgrades, stage stars, achievements, currency balances, and control settings—is stored locally on your device (using on-device browser/WebView storage and local backups).

Data Type Collected / Processed Storage / Provider Purpose
Personal Information (Name, Email, Contacts) No None Not requested or collected
Precise GPS Location / Camera / Microphone No None Not requested or collected
Game Progress & Settings Yes (Local Only) Local Device Storage Saves scores, unlocks, upgrades, currency, and preferences locally
Ad & Diagnostic Data (Optional Rewarded Ads) Yes (Optional) Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP-derived approximate location, ad events, and diagnostics for optional rewarded ads
Purchases & Achievements (Optional) Yes (Optional) Google Play Billing & Play Games Processes optional in-app purchases and syncs achievements

2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

To support optional rewarded videos, optional in-app purchases, and optional Google Play achievements on Android, Endless Arcade integrates the following Google services. Each service handles data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

A. Google AdMob (Optional Rewarded Ads Only)

Endless Arcade does not display forced interstitials or banner ads. Players may optionally choose to watch Rewarded Video Ads (for daily/weekly store supply drops, a 1× in-run revive, or a 2× post-run coin bonus).

  • SDK Used: Google Mobile Ads SDK (com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads)
  • Data Potentially Processed by Google: Advertising ID / Device or other IDs, approximate location (IP-derived), ad interaction events, and crash/diagnostic telemetry used for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement.
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google Uses Ad Data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

B. Google Play Billing (Optional In-App Purchases)

Optional Diamond packs and Google Play Points redemptions are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing (com.android.billingclient:billing). We never see or store your payment card or billing credentials.

C. Google Play Games Services

Optional player sign-in and achievement synchronization use Google Play Games Services (com.google.android.gms:play-services-games-v2).

3. Permissions Used (Android)

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Used to load optional rewarded ads, process optional Google Play Billing purchases, sync Play Games achievements, and open this Privacy Policy.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Required for optional in-app purchases via Google Play.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (merged via Google Mobile Ads SDK): Used by Google AdMob for optional rewarded ad delivery and fraud prevention.
  • VIBRATE: Used for tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (can be toggled off in Settings).

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, precise GPS location, contacts, or external files.

4. Children's Privacy

Endless Arcade is intended for a general audience (13 and older / Teen & General Audiences) and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service in connection with the app, please contact us at ashikhasnat30@gmail.com.

5. Your Choices & Data Deletion

  • Delete Local Save Data: You can permanently erase all locally stored game progress at any time via in-game Settings → Data & About → RESET SAVE DATA, or by clearing the app's storage / uninstalling Endless Arcade on your device.
  • Manage Ad Personalization & Advertising ID: You can opt out of personalized ads or reset/delete your Advertising ID anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Play Offline: Core gameplay modes remain playable offline without watching ads or making purchases.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as new features or SDKs are added. Any updates will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above and published at https://aashiqhasnat.github.io/privacy-policies/racer/.
