Privacy Policy for Endless Arcade

Last updated: September 7, 2026 • Package: com.arcade.racer

This Privacy Policy describes how Endless Arcade ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user data. The App is developed by Ashik Hasnat as an offline-first arcade racing game.

1. Information Collection and Use

We respect your privacy. Endless Arcade does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal identifying information (such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or real-time location).

Data Category Collected Storage Purpose
Personal Information No None Not required or requested
Device Location No None Not required or requested
Game Progress (Coins, Scores, Unlocks) Yes Local Device Storage To save your in-game progress locally
Preferences (Audio, Controls) Yes Local Device Storage To retain your gameplay settings

2. Local Game Storage

All game progression, vehicle unlocks, currency balances, high scores, and audio/control preferences are saved strictly on your local device. This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to external servers.

3. Local Multiplayer (LAN)

The App provides an optional Local Multiplayer feature. When you host or join a local race, communication happens strictly over your local Wi-Fi / local network between participating devices. No data or telemetry is transmitted to any external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or analytics services.

If you choose to use optional Google Play Services (such as Google Play Games or Google Play In-App Billing), those services are governed directly by Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not address anyone under the age of 13 for data collection purposes. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

6. Data Deletion and Control

You have full control over your saved data:

  • You can reset your game progress at any time inside the game via Settings → Data → Reset Progress.
  • You can delete all local data by clearing the app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
