Privacy Policy for Endless Arcade

Effective Date: September 7, 2026 • Application: Endless Arcade • Package Name: com.aashiqhasnat.endlessarcade • Developer: Ashik Hasnat

This Privacy Policy outlines how Endless Arcade ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") manages user information. The App is developed by Ashik Hasnat as an offline-first arcade racing game for mobile and desktop platforms.

1. Information Collection and Data Practices

We respect your digital privacy. Endless Arcade does not collect, record, transmit, store, or sell any personal identifying information (PII), such as your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, or precise GPS location.

Data Category Collected by Developer? Storage Location Primary Purpose
Personal Identifiers (Name, Email, Contacts) No None Not collected or requested
Device Location (GPS / Precise Coordinates) No None Not collected or required
Game Progression (Coins, Diamonds, Vehicle Unlocks) Yes (Locally Only) Local Device Storage To save your gameplay progress between game launches
User Preferences (Audio, Steering, Graphics) Yes (Locally Only) Local Device Storage To retain your personalized gameplay settings

2. Device Permissions Usage

The App requires minimal system permissions to operate standard gaming features:

  • Vibration / Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile vibration feedback during crashes, steering drift, and nitro acceleration.
  • Local Network Access (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / INTERNET): Used exclusively for optional Local Area Network (LAN) peer-to-peer multiplayer racing and future Google Play Services connections.

3. Local Storage & Data Security

All game progression, records, currency balances, vehicle customizations, and settings are saved strictly on your local device using standard operating system application sandbox storage. Because no personal data is transferred across the internet to developer servers, your gameplay data remains safe and self-contained on your hardware.

4. Local Multiplayer (LAN)

The App provides an optional Local Multiplayer mode. When hosting or joining a local multiplayer match:

  • Direct peer-to-peer socket communication occurs exclusively over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot network.
  • No gameplay, player identification, or network telemetry data is ever transmitted to external cloud servers.

5. Third-Party Services & Google Play

The App does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or behavioral profiling analytics.

If you utilize standard Google Play platform features (such as Google Play Games Achievements or Google Play In-App Billing for diamond packs), transactions and services are handled securely by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The App is designed for players of all ages and is family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The game contains no age-inappropriate content, behavioral tracking, or data-sharing mechanisms.

7. User Rights, Data Retention & Deletion

Because Endless Arcade does not require user accounts or store data on cloud servers, no account deletion request is necessary. You maintain total control over your local data:

  • In-Game Reset: You can purge all saved progression, high scores, and currency balances anytime via Settings → Data → Reset Progress.
  • Complete Deletion: Clearing the App's data/cache in your Android device settings or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local files immediately.

8. Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under regulations like the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), users have rights regarding their personal information. Because we do not collect, process, or sell personal data, your rights are inherently respected and protected by default.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect application updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated Effective Date.
