Privacy Policy

Application: Endless Arcade (Racer)  |  Package ID: com.arcade.racer  |  Effective Date: September 7, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Your privacy is paramount. Endless Arcade (referred to as "the Game", "we", "us", or "our") is designed as a self-contained, offline-first mobile and desktop arcade racing game developed by Ashik Hasnat.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal identifying information (PII). All gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device.

2. Information Collection & Storage

Here is an explicit breakdown of how your data is handled:

Data Type Collected? Storage Location Purpose
Personal Data (Name, Email, Phone, Contacts) No None Not collected or requested
Device Location (GPS, Precise Coordinates) No None Not collected or required
Game Progress (Coins, Diamonds, Unlocked Cars, High Scores) Yes (Locally) Local Device Storage To save your gameplay progress between sessions
Gameplay Settings (Audio volume, Controls, Graphics) Yes (Locally) Local Device Storage To retain your personalized preferences

3. Multiplayer & Local Network Connections

The game includes an optional Local Multiplayer Mode (LAN). When you host or join a local multiplayer race:

  • Network communications occur strictly over your local Wi-Fi / Local Area Network (LAN).
  • No gameplay, player identifiers, or network telemetry are ever sent to external cloud servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Endless Arcade does not integrate third-party advertising tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics, or location trackers.

If Google Play Services (such as Google Play Games Achievements or Google Play Billing for in-app purchases) are utilized, standard transaction and service data are processed securely by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is safe for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has inadvertently been stored or collected, please contact us immediately so we can assist.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all game state is stored locally on your device, you have complete and total control over your data at all times:

  • In-Game Reset: You can reset all game progress, scores, and currency anytime via Settings → Data → Reset Progress.
  • Uninstalling / App Data Clear: Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the game from your device permanently removes all saved files immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated Effective Date.
