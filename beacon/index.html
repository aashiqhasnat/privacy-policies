Privacy Policy

Application: Beacon Browser • Package ID: com.aashiqhasnat.beacon_browser • Last Updated: November 2026

Beacon Browser ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Ashik Hasnat, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure for the Beacon Browser Android application (com.aashiqhasnat.beacon_browser).

1. Privacy-First Design & Minimal Analytics

Beacon Browser is built with a privacy-first philosophy:

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, monetize, or sell your browsing history, visited URLs, search queries, bookmarks, cookies, or downloaded media files.
  • We do not display third-party ads or use behavioral advertising trackers.
  • No user account, registration, or cloud sign-in is required to use the browser.
  • Release builds include Google Firebase Analytics solely for basic, anonymous app-health and aggregate usage diagnostics (such as app launches, crash/stability signals, Android OS version, and Firebase installation/app instance identifiers). No browsing activity, URLs, Private Tab sessions, or downloaded filenames are ever sent to Firebase.

2. On-Device Local Data & Storage

All data generated during your usage of Beacon Browser stays exclusively on your local device:

  • Browsing History & Bookmarks: Stored securely within the application's local sandbox in an encrypted local database.
  • Cookies & Web Storage: Managed by the local Android system WebView and kept strictly within your private device sandbox.
  • Downloaded Media: Saved locally to your device's storage (via Android's standard Scoped MediaStore). You retain full control over all downloaded files.

3. Data Deletion & User Control

Because all data resides locally on your device, you have complete control over its deletion at any time:

  • You can wipe all browsing history, cache, and cookies directly inside the app via Settings → Clear Browsing Data.
  • Using Incognito / Private Tabs automatically discards all session cookies, cache, and history upon closing the private session.
  • You can erase all data by clearing app storage in Android System Settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Network Communications & Firebase

  • Direct Browsing: When navigating the web using Beacon Browser, network requests are made directly between your device and the websites you visit. We do not operate proxy servers, intermediate gateways, or monitor/log your traffic.
  • Firebase SDK (Google LLC): Release builds use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated app performance and usage metrics (app interactions, diagnostics, and device/app identifiers) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Browsing history, URLs, search queries, cookies, downloaded media, and Private Tab contents are never collected or transmitted.

5. Permissions Requested & Purpose

Beacon Browser requests only the permissions strictly necessary to deliver browser functionality, downloads, and media playback:

  • INTERNET: Required to browse webpages, stream media, and download files requested by the user.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Displays real-time download progress and active media player lock screen notification controls.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE (dataSync and mediaPlayback) & RUN_USER_INITIATED_JOBS (Android 14+): Ensures uninterrupted user-initiated file transfers and background audio/video playback with notification controls.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (Optional): Disabled by default. Requested only on-demand when a website prompts for geolocation (e.g. mapping), strictly requiring explicit per-site user consent. Never accessed in the background.
  • CAMERA & RECORD_AUDIO (Optional): Disabled by default. Requested only on-demand when a website prompts for camera or microphone input (e.g. video conferencing or QR scanning) with explicit user consent.

6. Ad & Tracker Blocking

Beacon Browser includes a built-in content blocker that blocks intrusive ads, tracking scripts, and popups using curated local filter lists. No browsing telemetry or blocked URL records are reported back to the developer or any third parties.

7. Google Play Policy Compliance & YouTube Restrictions

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies and YouTube's Terms of Service:

  • Beacon Browser strictly blocks and disables downloading or extracting video/audio streams from YouTube or Google-hosted video platforms.
  • Beacon Browser functions strictly as a standard web viewer for these services.
  • Users are solely responsible for ensuring they have appropriate rights before downloading content from authorized third-party sites.

8. Children's Privacy

Beacon Browser is a general-purpose web browser and does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected in this document and on our public privacy portal with a revised "Last updated" date.
